Import entries by code

The sampler of the other mine can send you the code of one entry (Code button in the history list) or of all their entries for the shift (button below), as a message or as a file. Paste the code(s) here or open the file. The quality, ore type, destination and scheduled % are added to your lists and shift report. Photos and maps are not copied. Imported entries show a plain text card, and you enter the trips as usual.
